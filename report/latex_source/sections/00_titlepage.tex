\vspace{6mm}
{\Large A Unified Framework for Quaternion-Modelled Hexacopters\\[1mm]
and Fixed-Wing Aircraft with Constrained\\[1mm]
Moving-Horizon Estimation and Model-Predictive Control\par}
\vspace{4mm}
{\color{accent}\rule{\linewidth}{1.2pt}}\\[1.4cm]

{\large Technical Report\par}
\vspace{0.9cm}

\begin{minipage}{0.85\linewidth}\centering\itshape\small
Complete first-principles derivations of a hexacopter model with full
quaternion attitude dynamics and of a six-degree-of-freedom fixed-wing model,
together with a distributed moving-horizon estimation (DMHE) and distributed
model-predictive control (DMPC) architecture built around a real-time
constrained nonlinear MPC controller and a real-time constrained moving-horizon
estimator running as the per-agent computational core.
\end{minipage}
\vspace{1.6cm}
